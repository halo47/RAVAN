# RAVAN — Travel Company User Guide (`COMPANY_USER_GUIDE.md`)

## 1. Getting Started & Onboarding
- Sign in using Google Authentication on the RAVAN workspace.
- Create your travel company profile (`ناوی کۆمپانیا`, `Slug`, `Logo`, `Phone`, `Default Currency: IQD or USD`) or click **ئامادەکردنی داتای تاقیکردنەوە** to load sample tenants.
- Follow the 8-step **Onboarding Checklist** on the main Dashboard (`داشبۆردی سەرەکی`).

## 2. Managing Trips & Exclusive Registration Links
- Open **گەشتەکان (Trips)** and click **+ دروستکردنی گەشتی نوێ**.
- Upload a cover photo directly from your device or choose a preset, set price (`IQD` or `USD`), seat capacity, and pickup point.
- Every trip has its own **Exclusive Single-Trip Link (`/?trip=CODE`)**. Click **کۆپی لینک** to share on WhatsApp/Instagram/Facebook. When customers open that link, they see **only** that trip and its participant registration form.
- To share all published trips of your company on a single branded agency page, click **پەڕەی گشتیی کۆمپانیا (`/?org=SLUG`)** in the sidebar.

## 3. Managing Bookings & Passenger Manifests
- Open **حجزکردنەکان (Bookings)** to view all online and manual reservations.
- Click any booking code (`RVN-...`) to inspect the full **Passenger Manifest** (full names, age groups, phone numbers) and **Status Audit History**.
- Approve (`پەسەندکردن`) or Cancel (`هەڵوەشاندنەوە`) bookings; seats are automatically recalculated.

## 4. Trip Preparation & Live Passenger Attendance
- On any trip card in **گەشتەکان**, click **ئامادەکاری و ئامادەبوون (Trip Operations & Manifest)**.
- Toggle the 8-item **Preparation Checklist** (Bus, Guide, Hotel, Meals, Permits, Manifest, Emergency Contacts, Departure Notice).
- Assign a Guide and Vehicle, record operational notes, and mark each passenger as **ئامادەیە (Present)** or **نەهاتوو (No-Show)**.
- Click **داگرتنی لیستی گەشتیاران (CSV)** to export the manifest for checkpoints and tour leaders.

## 5. Dual-Currency Finance (`IQD` & `USD`)
- Open **دارایی و قازانج (Finance)** to record customer payments/installments (`+ تۆمارکردنی پارەدان`) and trip expenses (`+ تۆمارکردنی خەرجی`).
- `IQD` and `USD` ledgers are strictly separated—never mixed.

## 6. Subscription, Reports & Support
- Open **بەشداری و پسووڵەکان** to view your plan limits, apply discount coupons (`RAVAN20`), submit renewal references, and print official invoices (`INV-RVN-...`).
- Open **ڕاپۆرت و پاڵپشتی** to export CSV reports or open a support ticket with the RAVAN team.
